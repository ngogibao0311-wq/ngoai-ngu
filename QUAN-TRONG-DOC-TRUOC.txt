CACH MO WEB DE GOOGLE FIREBASE DANG NHAP DUOC

1. KHONG nhap dup index.html.
2. Nhap dup MO-WEB-DUNG-CACH.cmd.
3. Trinh duyet phai hien dia chi: http://localhost:5174/
4. Firebase Console > Authentication > Sign-in method > Google: Enable.
5. Firebase Console > Authentication > Settings > Authorized domains: phai co "localhost".
   Chi them localhost, KHONG them http://localhost:5174.

Neu thanh dia chi cua trinh duyet bat dau bang file:/// thi ban dang mo sai cach.
